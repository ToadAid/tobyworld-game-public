STUDY THE LAND

HOW TO TEND

  • Move with swipes, or hold the directional pad, arrow keys, or WASD. Release to stop on the next tile.
  • Remain still on every sprout and hold Patience to tend it.
  • Stop beside the Keeper and hold Patience to receive the land's optional blessing.
  • At the bottom-center gate, use Yield and move across while the leaves are open.
  • Watch living paths breathe open and closed; holding Patience slows their cycle.
  • Cross ember paths on the cool tide; Patience slows the heat, and the Ancient Rune cools it forever.
  • When the Kiribako wakes, revisit its numbered memory echoes in order.
  • Find the Gate Key to permanently open marked shortcut doors across the Meadow.
  • Recover the Lore Map to chart a persistent route toward unfinished objectives.
  • In the veiled Stillwater, listen to Hush, then stop completely and hold Patience to reveal submerged paths.
  • At the Pond threshold, stop beside a ring and hold Patience to open it briefly—or listen to Satoby to be welcomed permanently.
  • In the final Pond, find the Wayfinder, then stop on each Door Echo and hold Patience until all three answer.
  • Find each deed's Relic, gather its Lore Lights, and return to the Core.
  • Harvest the purple Taboshi Bloom to eat ghosts for seven seconds; eaten ghosts return far from Toby. This is a Toadaid-adapted game mechanic.
  • Use a controller with the D-pad or left stick to move, A to hold Patience or confirm, B to Yield, and Menu/Start to pause. In menus, use any direction to move focus and A to choose.

A CLEAR BOUNDARY

ABOUT THIS WORLD

Tobyworld: Paths of Patience is a Toadaid-created game adaptation built around canonical Lore Land Deed references.

CANON & ADAPTATION

Deed numbers, five signs, canonical metadata, and canonical artwork are presented as source references. Playable worlds, layouts, objectives, dialogue, music, and generated sandbox rules are Toadaid adaptations—not claims about canonical lore or deed utility.

PRIVACY

Campaign progress, best scores, control introduction, and haptic preference stay in this browser's local storage. There is no account, wallet connection, analytics, advertising tracker, or automatic social posting.

Archive visuals load locally first. If a local image is unavailable, the game may request an IPFS gateway fallback, which receives ordinary web request information such as an IP address. Visited assets may be cached on this device for offline play.

SHARING

Journey Cards and social sharing are future experiments. If added, cards will be created locally and sharing will require an explicit player action; nothing will be posted automatically.

CREDITS

Game design, playable treatments, code, Keeper dialogue, and generative audio: Toadaid. Canonical Lore Land Deed records and imagery remain visibly separated from adapted game material.
