## Excluded categories reviewed at the source repository

| Requested category | Source/creator record | Ownership or permission evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canonical deed artwork | Canonical Tobyworld archive; individual creator/license records not present | Possession, hashes, and NFT ownership do not establish redistribution rights | Excluded |
| Imagined Lore Land artwork | Described as ToadAid interpretations; generation model, inputs, and terms incomplete | Insufficient record to establish clean redistribution | Excluded |
| Character artwork | Existing Toby, Taboshi, Patience, and group image files | No complete author/license record found | Excluded |
| Keeper artwork | Generated/game-ready files with technical manifests | Hashes and dimensions prove integrity, not copyright permission | Excluded |
| Canonical metadata and descriptions | Tobyworld deed metadata archive | No repository-wide redistribution grant found | Excluded |
| Generated visual derivatives | Thumbnails and responsive derivatives from unresolved masters | Format conversion does not create a new redistribution right | Excluded |
| Community-contributed material | No cleared contribution ledger supplied for this candidate | Must be reviewed file by file under the contribution policy | Excluded until documented |

## Names, lore, and marks

Tobyworld, Toadgang, Toadgod, Lore Land Deeds, associated characters, lore, and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Their limited appearance in community links and explanatory notices is descriptive and does not imply ownership, endorsement, or a reuse license.

Do not import content from the full production repository into this edition merely because both projects share code or maintainers. Any future asset needs creator, source, date/method, derivative chain, explicit redistribution permission, applicable license, and required attribution before inclusion.
