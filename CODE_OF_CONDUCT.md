# Community Code of Conduct

This project should feel like a welcoming pond: curious questions are welcome, patient collaboration matters, and nobody needs game-development or crypto experience to participate.

## Our standard

Be kind, specific, and constructive. Respect different identities, backgrounds, experience levels, access needs, and communication styles. Give credit, preserve provenance, and ask before reusing another person's work. Separate disagreement about an idea from judgment of a person, and keep canon, adaptation, and speculation clearly labeled.

Harassment, threats, hateful or sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, scams, wallet solicitation, token promotion, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact a ToadAid maintainer through an established private community channel and ask for a confidential reporting route before sharing details. If immediate safety is at risk, contact an appropriate local service.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation to protect the community. Reports should be handled with discretion, fairness, and respect.
