# Community Code of Conduct

Tobyworld: Paths of Patience should feel like a welcoming pond: curious questions are welcome, patient collaboration matters, and no frog needs prior game-development or crypto knowledge to participate.

## Our standard

We ask contributors and maintainers to:

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, access needs, and communication styles.
- Give credit, preserve provenance, and ask before reusing another person's work.
- Separate disagreement about an idea from judgment of a person.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and leave room for learning.
- Keep canon, adaptation, and speculation clearly labeled.

Harassment, threats, hateful or sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Neither are scams, wallet solicitation, token promotion, or pressure to reveal financial information in project spaces.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact a ToadAid maintainer through an established private channel and ask for a confidential conduct-reporting route before sharing details. If immediate safety is at risk, contact the appropriate local service rather than waiting for a project response.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect the community. Reports should be handled with discretion, fairness, and respect for everyone involved.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, community events, and other spaces where someone is representing the project or ToadAid.
